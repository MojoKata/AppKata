# R17.11. Where permissions come from

R17.11. **Where permissions come from.** In order: the user's per-app choice (install options R4.9, App
Detail) wins; then the merged reviews' `Sandbox*` lines (R10.3.1, resolved per R17.3.2); then the default
template (R17.3.3). Reviews **never** change an app's grants on their own: a new version installs
normally and keeps the user's current grants, and if its reviews suggest more or less access (anything
the app would run with differently, its own MPRIS name, R17.5.1, included, though no summary chip shows
that) the app gets a standing **review badge** (not a blocked update), and the Sandbox dialog offers
the suggestion ("Use theirs"). The badge names what the suggestion adds and
removes; with more access it says the app may not work fully until reviewed, with less that the app
may have more access than it needs (review 2's nit: it listed the whole suggestion and said neither).
Access is never silently widened. The badge stands until the user
**acts on that suggestion**: adopts it (one click, in the badge's Review… dialog; a suggested Off is
adopted as the user's own choice, R17.3.2), keeps their current
grants ("Keep mine"), or saves a sandbox choice of their own knowing it. A later, different
suggestion raises it again; a user's deliberate choice never leaves a permanent badge.
