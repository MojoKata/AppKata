# R17.15. Settings → Sandbox

R17.15. **Settings → Sandbox** (R11.1): the default template for new apps (by kind, R17.3.3); the
**System support** status and one-time **Set up…** (R17.10), or **Repair…** where AppKata's profile sits
beside the system's own (R17.10.2); "Follow reviewers' suggestions"; the
review-badge toggle (R17.11); a read-only **Built-in templates** list; the sandbox home folders; and the
session-type warning (R17.9) shown prominently. "Follow reviewers' suggestions" **off** takes the
reviews out of sandboxing: neither their `Sandbox` lines nor the reviewed baseline of R17.3.3 apply,
so an app with no choice of the user's own gets the default template for its kind, and the review
badge never shows. The Sandbox dialog still says what the reviewers suggest, and "Use theirs" stays
available as an explicit choice.
