The steps

Each step of the sweep is an issue, and its findings are its sub-issues. A step is checked when its issue is closed, and then shows what changed in its areas at its pull request's merge. Click a step for its findings.

Findings: closedHigh or Med, open Low or unrated, openquestion

By area

Each area of the coverage page, the same as the area: labels. A finding counts in the area its label names, else in its step's. Click an area for its findings. Mutation: the changes mutmut made to the area's code and how many no test caught, from its latest record; baseline, changed since when a module has changed since that record or isn't in it, so the figures are out of date.
